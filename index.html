    How to play

    1. Grab anything (a book, a plate, a remote) or nothing at all, and hold it up with both hands like a steering wheel.
    2. Lock on: get comfortable, hold steady for a second, then turn left and right once.
    3. Turn it to steer. The kart drives itself.
    4. Flick it to use your item: a quick snap of your wrists or a quick pop up.
    5. Raise it and hold to start a race (or press Space).
    PlayersUp to 4. Up to 2 on the camera, standing side by side (P1 on the left, P2 on the right). Everyone else uses a keyboard or a gamepad.
    KeyboardsTwo people can share one: one on WASD, one on the arrow keys.
    CameraKeep both hands in view. If it can't see them, the race pauses and carries on when they're back. Your video never leaves your device.
    KeysEsc pause · F full screen · R recenter steering · C camera view · M mute · D diagnostics

    Items

    Drive through a crate to get one (you hold one at a time), then flick to use it. Racers further back get stronger items.

    Paused

    Esc or P pause, F full screen, M mute, C camera size, R recenter, D diagnostics

    Can't see your hands

    Bring both hands back into view and the race carries on by itself.
